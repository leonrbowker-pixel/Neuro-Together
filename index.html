Sensory Body Battery Check-In

Assess current nervous system capacity. Selecting a level explains your child's regulation state and offers a recommended schedule to prevent meltdowns.

🟢 100% Full — Optimal Nervous System Bandwidth Peak Capacity

What this means: High sensory and emotional resilience. Executive function is fully primed; transitions, social interactions, and academic tasks require low co-regulation.

Parent Strategy: Best window for new skills, noisy environments, haircuts, medical visits, or trying new foods.

Suggested Visual Schedule for Today
1. Morning: Full Morning Prep + Challenge
2. Midday: High Focus Learning / Errand
3. Afternoon: High-Energy Heavy Play
4. Evening: Normal Bedtime Routine
🔵 75% Steady — Balanced & Functioning Well Steady State

What this means: Handles familiar routines with ease, but may show subtle signs of fatigue if multiple sensory inputs stack up without quiet breaks.

Parent Strategy: Keep the day predictable. Pair challenging activities directly with preferred sensory breaks.

Suggested Visual Schedule for Today
1. Morning: Standard Visual Routine
2. Midday: Task + 15m Quiet Solo Break
3. Afternoon: Low-Demand Sensory Play
4. Evening: Dim-Light Wind Down
🟡 50% Paced — Sensory Reserves Depleting Caution / Pacing

What this means: Working memory and self-regulation are running on reserve. Noticeable vocal stimming, restlessness, zoning out, or shorter patience.

Parent Strategy: Drop non-essential expectations. Introduce proprioceptive input (weighted lap pad, trampolining, compression hug) to recharge.

Suggested Visual Schedule for Today
1. Morning: Essential Tasks Only
2. Midday: Proprioceptive Reset & Snack
3. Afternoon: Special Interest Immersion
4. Evening: Early Wind-Down & Low Light
🔴 25% Survival Mode — Overload & Meltdown Prevention Safety / Low Demand

What this means: The nervous system has shifted into fight, flight, or shutdown mode. Verbal reasoning, multi-step requests, or corrective reprimands will provoke an explosive meltdown.

Parent Strategy: Immediate sensory retreat. Reduce ambient noise, dim screens, eliminate direct demands, and provide co-regulation through calm proximity.

Suggested Visual Schedule for Today
1. Safe Haven: Sensory Cave / Dark Room
2. Nourishment: Safe Food & Drink (Zero Pressure)
3. Comfort: Passive Calming (Ear Defenders/Weighted Pad)
4. Rest: Sleep / Deep Decompression

UK Special Educational Needs Law

UK EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) Master Guide

An EHCP is a legally binding document issued by your Local Authority (LA) under the Children and Families Act 2014. It legally mandates specified educational provision that mainstream school budgets cannot provide alone.

The Statutory 20-Week Legal Timeline

Week 0–6: Request & Decision LA has 6 weeks from your parental request to agree or refuse to assess.
Week 6–16: Assessment Evidence gathering from EP, Speech & Language, OT, and medical reports.
Week 16: Draft Plan LA issues Draft EHCP or notifies refusal to issue (triggers immediate tribunal appeal rights).
Week 20: Final Legal Plan Final EHCP legally sealed, naming specific school placement and funded hours.

Understanding & Checking Sections A to K:

Section A: Views of Parent & Child

The child's story, aspirations, and what an ordinary day looks like. Highlight sensory overwhelm, school anxiety, and masking behaviours.

Section B: Special Educational Needs (SEN)

Every single challenge (sensory processing, executive dysfunction, working memory, emotional regulation) MUST be written here. If it is not in Section B, the council does not have to fund it in Section F.

Section F: Special Educational Provision (The Golden Section)

Must be quantified and specified by law. Reject vague phrasing like "regular support from a TA" or "access to sensory breaks". Insist on exact details: "1-to-1 Teaching Assistant for 25 hours per week" or "3x 15-minute scheduled occupational sensory diet breaks daily".

Section I: Placement / School Named

Names the specific school (mainstream, enhanced resourced provision, or independent specialist). Left blank in the draft for parental preference consultation.
